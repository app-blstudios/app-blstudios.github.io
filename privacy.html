Privacy Policy

Totally Legal Climb · BL Studios · Effective: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Totally Legal Climb (the "Game"), made by BL Studios ("we", "us"). In short: we don't run any servers and don't collect your personal data ourselves. Your progress stays on your device. The Game shows optional ads through Google AdMob, and Google handles the data needed for those ads as described below.

1. Who we are

BL Studios, Slovenia. Contact: support.blstudios@gmail.com

2. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress and settings locally on your device: best height, coins, unlocked climbers, missions, achievements, daily streaks, sound/vibration settings and whether you accepted these documents. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. There are no accounts, no chat and no in-game purchases.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch an ad to revive or to get coins. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers,
  • IP address (which can indicate approximate location),
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads shown, watched, tapped) and diagnostic data.

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. Without consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which use data only as needed for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Your controls on Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Game engine (Unity)

The Game is made with Unity. Unity Technologies may process limited technical data (such as device type, operating system and performance or crash information) to operate the engine. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

6. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Personalised advertising is based on your consent. Delivering, measuring and securing ads and keeping the Game working rely on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country, for example 16 in some EU countries). We do not knowingly allow ad partners to collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help to have it removed.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object, to withdraw consent and to complain to a data protection authority. As we do not hold your personal data ourselves, data used for ads is held by Google; you can manage it through the controls above and Google's tools. For any question, contact us and we will help.

9. International transfers and retention

Google and Unity may process data outside your country, using safeguards required by law (such as Standard Contractual Clauses). They keep data according to their own policies. We keep no personal data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. When changes are important, the Game will ask you to accept the new version. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
